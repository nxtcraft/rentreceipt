When Are Rent Receipts Needed?

  • HRA exemption > ₹1L/year: Landlord PAN mandatory; receipts required by employer.
  • HRA exemption ≤ ₹1L/year: Receipts still recommended as proof.
  • Self-employed / ITR filing: Rent paid as home office expense may need receipts.

What a Valid Rent Receipt Must Contain

  • Tenant's full name
  • Landlord's full name, address and PAN (if annual rent > ₹1L)
  • Property address
  • Amount paid (₹)
  • Period covered (month and year)
  • Date of receipt
  • Landlord's signature
  • Revenue stamp (₹1) if payment in cash > ₹5,000

Revenue Stamp Rules

A ₹1 revenue stamp is needed only if rent is paid in cash and exceeds ₹5,000 per receipt. For digital payments (bank transfer, UPI, NEFT, IMPS), no revenue stamp is required. Most employers accept digital-payment receipts without stamps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need rent receipts for HRA exemption?

If annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh, your employer requires receipts with the landlord's PAN to allow HRA exemption. Without them, TDS is deducted on the full HRA. Keep receipts even for smaller amounts as proof if your return is scrutinised.

Does a rent receipt need a revenue stamp?

Only for cash payments above ₹5,000 per receipt. Digital payments (UPI, NEFT, bank transfer) do not require revenue stamps.

Does a landlord need to file taxes on rent?

Yes. Rent is taxable as "Income from House Property". Standard 30% deduction for maintenance, municipal taxes paid and home loan interest (Section 24b) can be claimed. Net rental income is added to total income and taxed at slab rate.
